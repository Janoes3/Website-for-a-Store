Likert Scales 



Use Top‑2 / Bottom‑2 analysis (EXAM‑FRIENDLY)
This is straight from your DPDD slides.

Top‑2 = Positive experience (Agree + Strongly Agree)
Bottom‑2 = Negative experience (Disagree + Strongly Disagree)

What examiners look for here:

Trends
Skews (mostly positive? mixed?)
Outliers (a few low scores)

Avoid statements like:
“Most users liked it.”

Instead say:
“The majority of users rated this feature positively, with a small proportion indicating difficulty.”
